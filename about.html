HOW IT BEGAN

Built around
the in-between.

The pause between one thing and the next. The familiar walk to the counter. The first unhurried conversation of the day.

Ellora was imagined as a neighbourhood living room: generous, thoughtful, and easy to return to. Since 2018, we have put care into the small things—from the coffee we source to the way we remember your usual.
