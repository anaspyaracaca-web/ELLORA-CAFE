Ask us something else ↗
Do you take walk-ins?

Absolutely. Come on in and we’ll find a spot if we can. If you have a particular time in mind, you can also send a reservation request on our demo booking page.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

We do our best to offer thoughtful options, and recipes can change with the season. Please speak with our team before ordering so we can talk through ingredients and allergen information.

Can I bring my laptop?

Of course. We love a good work-from-café day. At busy times, we may ask everyone to keep the larger tables free for groups.

Do you host gatherings?

We do enjoy bringing people together. Visit our events page and tell us a little about the occasion to start a conversation.

Is the menu the same every day?

Our coffee favourites stick around, but the kitchen follows the season. The online menu is a sample; ask the team what’s especially lovely today.

Can I order Ellora catering?

We can help with catering enquiries. Get in touch with a date, an approximate guest count, and a few details about your gathering.
